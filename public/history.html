About these numbers

  • Close: the last reading of the day. Readings are every 15 minutes from 7:00 to 21:00 and hourly overnight. From November 2023 to November 2024 and from November 2025 to September 2026 there's one value per day. Before November 2024: Blue and official buy/sell (from 2011) and MEP (from October 2018) come from ArgentinaDatos; CCL (one price, from 2010) and the official sell before 2011 from a daily history we downloaded; Visa and Mastercard start in November 2023. Blank means no value that day.
  • Western Union (est.): since December 2021, on days without a WU rate we show CCL buy, which WU tracks closely: within 0.2% at the median since November 2024, and within about 1% on real transfers from December 2021 to October 2023 (shown as actual values). On days when CCL jumps, WU can lag by a day, so the estimate runs high. Before December 2021 WU is blank.
  • Visa (est.): the previous day's MEP sell × 0.935 (Visa has run about 6.5% below the previous day's MEP in every half-year since late 2023); Sunday and Monday keep Saturday's rate, as Visa does. Not estimated before November 2023: earlier, foreign cards were charged nearer the official rate.
  • Mastercard (est.): the same day's Visa on weekdays (they match); Saturday and Sunday keep Friday's rate, as Mastercard does.
  • AMEX (est.): the Central Bank's A3500 rate from two business days before, which is how AMEX converts peso charges (on real charges, the median difference was 0.05%). Days with a real charge show that rate as actual. It starts at the earliest real charge we have (August 2025): before currency controls ended in April 2025, A3500 was far below the rate foreign cards were charged, so it can't stand in for AMEX.
  • A reading far off the rest of its day or week (a bad scrape) is left out. See how we get these rates for sources.
