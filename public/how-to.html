How to Use ARS Rates

Last reviewed: November 2026

Videos: ARS Rates overview · Reading the rates table

📈 Reading the rates table

  • Rate names: tap or hover over any rate name for what it is and how it's calculated.
  • Buy/Sell: rates with two prices show buy (what you get selling dollars) and sell (what you pay buying dollars).
  • VS 24H: the change from about 24 hours earlier.
  • VS BLUE: how each rate compares with Blue buy. Green means more pesos per dollar than Blue, red means fewer.
  • (Est.): an estimate, shown until the real rate is out. Mastercard is estimated from Visa until it publishes around 3 PM ET. AMEX is estimated from BCRA's Mayorista rate.
  • "as of" time: that rate didn't update on the latest check, so it shows when it last did.
  • Quick comparison: the link next to "Pesos per 1 USD" shows what a 100,000-peso bill costs in US dollars with Western Union, AMEX, cash dollars, Visa and Mastercard at the current rates. See also how the fee and the gap to Blue decide Western Union vs cash.
  • Updates: every 15 minutes from 7 AM to 9 PM Argentina time, and hourly overnight.

📍 Western Union tools

  • Find a WU Location: search by address or zip code, CABA neighborhood or province, filter by the day and time you want to go, and see the nearest first. "WU branches only" shows WU's own offices, which usually have cash, and "Had cash here?" lets you report what you found.
  • How to Get Pesos with WU: setup, keeping the cost down, and what to bring.
